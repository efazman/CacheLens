=== Adjudication ===

(a) "concentration ranking places the index-update line at #1": FAILS AS LITERALLY
STATED, in an explicable way. Neither store_tail (line 153, wilson_lb=0.223 shared)
nor store_head (line 156, wilson_lb=0.326 shared) ranks #1 -- both sit in the lower
half of the table. The actual #1 among statistically reliable (order-10^4-sample)
sites is line 182 (pop's spin-wait check, wilson_lb=0.532). But line 157 -- the
single instruction immediately after store_head's real store -- ranks a close #2-3
(wilson_lb=0.519, on 21,440 pooled access samples, not a low-n fluke) AND shows by
far the largest RELATIVE response to the false-sharing condition of any line in the
table: +673.5%, from a padded-build value (0.067) near the very bottom of the table
to a shared-build value in the top 3. This is the cleanest single piece of evidence
in this dataset that the effect is real and specifically localized: it is one x86
instruction downstream of the literal write this experiment is about, consistent
with `precise_ip=0`'s unbounded-but-typically-small skid (this project has
previously measured 99.99% within +-2 lines on a different workload) landing
one instruction past the actual event rather than exactly on it.

(b) "raw miss-count ranking does not place the index-update line at #1": true, but
not meaningfully confirmatory the way it was meant to be. Raw count's #1 (line 166)
is a THIRD line, distinct from both the literal index-update lines and from
concentration's own #1 (line 182) -- concentration and raw count do disagree with
each other, reproducing this project's core methodological point on a second,
structurally different workload, but neither one isolates the specific bug as
cleanly as Gate 5's matrix result did.

(c) "the padded queue shows neither the wall-clock cost nor the concentration
signal": CONFIRMED for wall-clock (padded is 2.75x-3.62x faster, every measurement).
PARTIALLY CONFIRMED for concentration: 14 of 16 reliably-sampled lines show a real,
positive increase under contention (median +58%), consistent with false sharing
creating broad memory-system pressure rather than a perfectly localized signal --
but the single largest and cleanest differential (line 157, +673.5%) sits exactly
where the mechanism predicts it should, one instruction from the literal write.
Two lines (164, 188) show a negative or flat relative change; both have small
pooled sample counts (164: 748-9628 access; 188: 217-1840) and are read as noise,
not as evidence against the effect, consistent with why the Wilson bound and the
30-sample gate exist in the first place.

=== What this changes about how the prediction can be claimed ===

Gate 4's skid characterization ("99.99% within +-2 lines") was measured on a
7-instruction, 2-source-line hot loop body -- almost the entire function was the
hot line, so skid had almost nowhere else to land. This workload's push()/pop()
span 10+ source lines each, giving skid real room to move, and it visibly does:
the strongest signal in this dataset sits one line away from the literal target,
not on it. The honest reading is not "concentration failed to find the bug" -- it
is "concentration found a site immediately adjacent to the bug, and a raw-count
ranking of the same run would not have."
